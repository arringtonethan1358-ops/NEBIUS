"Implied bar" means what H2 2026 has to deliver for the full-year guidance (reaffirmed in August) to hold. Each bar is our arithmetic, H2 = FY guide − H1 actual. The H1 actuals come from the Q2 2026 6-K, Exhibit 99.2.

## Questions for the call

1. **Microsoft ramp:** how much of the Q3 increase came from Microsoft volumes, and what remains for Q4?
2. **Pricing:** what share of revenue is on-demand or short-term, i.e. exposed to the October 1 price increase? How did the September auction contracts price against the $20–25M per MW on core deals?
3. **Software layer:** is there any revenue or usage metric for the inference and agent products built on the Tavily, ClarifAI and Eigen AI acquisitions?
4. **Funding:** after the August converts, how much of the 2027 capex is already funded by prepayments versus still needing new capital?
5. **H100 pricing:** is demand for older Hopper GPUs holding as Blackwell capacity comes online?
6. **Price basis:** are the per-MW prices quoted per IT MW or per grid MW, and are they annual? Even the 5-year tier (~$12M) is above the ~$7–11M of ARR per connected MW implied by guidance (see `research/nebius-initiation.md` §7.3).
7. **Live capacity:** how many MW were active vs. connected at September 30? The author's estimate of ~200–275 MW active implies year-end ARR below $7B unless revenue per MW is near the tier prices.
8. **SBC:** Eigen AI's $508.1M of retention stock vests over 4 years (~$127M a year). What's the go-forward SBC run-rate including Inferize?
