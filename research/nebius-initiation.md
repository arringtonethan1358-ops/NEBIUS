# Nebius Group (NASDAQ: NBIS) — Initiation Note

*As of 2026-10-11. Latest reported quarter: Q2 2026 (reported 2026-08-12). Next catalyst: Q3 2026 results (expected mid-November 2026).*

> **Not investment advice.** This is independent research for educational purposes. Figures are sourced inline; see [Source quality](#source-quality) before relying on any number.

---

## TL;DR

- **What it is:** A neocloud. Nebius builds and runs GPU data centers and rents AI compute (training and inference) through its own cloud platform. AI cloud was **98% of Q2 2026 revenue**. [^q2pr]
- **Where it is:** Q2 2026 revenue was **$582.3M, up 454% YoY**. ARR reached **$3.0B**, up from $1.9B in March and $1.25B at the end of 2025. Group adjusted EBITDA margin was **41%**, and the AI cloud margin was **50%**. [^q2pr] [^q1]
- **What it's guiding to (2026, reaffirmed in August):** $3.0–3.4B revenue, **$7–9B year-end ARR**, ~40% adjusted EBITDA margin, and **$20–25B capex**. The year-end contracted power target was raised to **5 GW**. [^q2pr] [^q2letter]
- **The debate:** Growth and backlog are real, with large Microsoft and Meta contracts and ~$37.5B of reported RPO. [^risks] Against that, Nebius is spending 3–4x its operating cash flow on capex. It funds the gap with customer prepayments, convertible notes and some equity, and three customers are ~59% of revenue. [^risks]
- **Our framing:** The stock is a bet on **converting contracted power into connected, revenue-generating megawatts on schedule**, financed without crippling dilution. The KPIs below are built to track exactly that.

## 1. Business overview

| Segment | What it does | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **Nebius AI cloud** (core) | GPU clusters, proprietary cloud software, and managed tools for training and inference | ~98% of revenue; the growth engine [^q2pr] |
| **Avride** | Autonomous driving and delivery robots | Majority-owned; ownership % unverified [^biz] |
| **TripleTen** | Edtech / tech re-skilling | Consolidated, small [^biz] |
| **ClickHouse** (equity stake) | Open-source analytics database company | Minority stake (~28% per one source, unverified). Its revaluation drove Q1 2026 GAAP net income of $621M [^q1] [^biz] |
| **Toloka** (equity stake) | AI training data | Majority *economic* interest per one source [^biz] |

**Why the non-core assets matter:** they are optionality the market tends to ignore. ClickHouse in particular can produce large non-cash gains or losses (as in Q1 2026) and is a potential source of cash if Nebius sells some of the stake.

Sources: [^q2pr] [^q1] [^risks]. Q4 2025 revenue is derived from Q1's reported "+75% QoQ" ($399M / 1.75). Check it against the Q4 6-K.

**Read-through:**
1. **Operating leverage is showing up.** The AI cloud margin went from 24% to 45% to 50% in three quarters. The ~40% full-year group guide looks conservative if 2H mix holds, though new capacity coming online carries start-up costs.
2. **The 2H ramp is huge.** Getting from $3.0B ARR (June) to $7–9B (December) means adding $4–6B in two quarters, more than all ARR added in the company's history. Management ties this to the **Microsoft ramp in Q3 and Q4** and new capacity coming online. [^capex] This is the single most important thing to verify in Q3.
3. **GAAP will lag adjusted EBITDA** for years. Depreciation on $20B+ of annual capex will swamp GAAP earnings even as cash margins expand. Value it on EBITDA, ARR and cash flow, not P/E.

**A sanity check we run each quarter (derived):** if the $7–9B ARR target rests on 0.8–1.0 GW of connected power, it implies roughly **$7–11M of ARR per connected MW**. Track this ratio. A falling ratio means pricing pressure or slow utilization. A rising one means the mix is shifting toward higher-value inference and software. *(Our derivation, not a company metric. The company's "$20–25M per MW" figure appears to be contract value per MW, not annual revenue; confirm the definition.)*

**Why it matters:** prepayments are the cheapest capital Nebius has, because customers effectively fund their own GPUs. The higher the share of capex funded by prepayments and asset-backed debt (like the July facility), the less dilutive equity and converts are needed. **Prepayments as a share of capex** is the funding KPI to watch.

## 6. Bull case

1. **Supply-constrained market.** Nebius sold out its capacity in Q1 [^q1t], and hyperscalers are paying multi-year, prepaid contracts for it.
2. **Margins are scaling.** The AI cloud adjusted EBITDA margin hit 50% in Q2, and there is room to expand as fixed platform costs spread over more GW.
3. **Visibility.** ~$37.5B RPO plus a 5 GW contracted-power pipeline backs multi-year revenue.
4. **Lower-risk capital structure than it looks.** Prepayments and asset-backed facilities fund a large share of capex.
5. **Hidden assets.** ClickHouse, Avride and Toloka add sum-of-the-parts value that the AI-cloud multiple does not capture.

## 7. Bear case

1. **Execution and power delivery.** Turning 5 GW contracted into connected MW depends on construction partners, grid interconnects and GPU deliveries. Past partner delays have happened. [^risks]
2. **Dilution.** Over $9B of converts were issued in 2026 alone, plus at-the-market (ATM) share sales and note-for-share exchanges. Per one analysis, the converts' fair value (~$20.8B) far exceeds their book value (~$8.5B), signalling heavy in-the-money dilution. [^risks]
3. **Customer concentration.** About 59% of revenue comes from three customers. A renegotiation or capacity reallocation by Microsoft or Meta would be material. [^risks]
4. **GPU obsolescence.** Depreciation schedules may prove too long if each GPU generation erodes the rental pricing of the last one.
5. **Hyperscaler insourcing.** Big customers are also building their own capacity, so neocloud demand may be cyclical overflow rather than structural.

## 8. Valuation framework

Not a price target. A framework to fill in with live market data:

- **EV / forward ARR:** EV = market cap + debt (including converts at fair value) − cash. Compare against year-end ARR guidance ($7–9B) and against peers such as CoreWeave.
- **EV / 2027E adjusted EBITDA:** with a ~40% group margin on a 2027 revenue run-rate.
- **Sum of the parts:** AI cloud (EV/EBITDA) + ClickHouse stake (last private round) + Avride and Toloka (book value or last round) − net debt.
- **Fully diluted share count:** always include convertible conversion shares. With converts this large, basic share count understates the denominator.
